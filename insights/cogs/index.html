Receipts

Ingredients

Costs are ex GST, per base unit, from confirmed receipts. "Counted" items are counted at the fortnightly stocktake; the rest (spices, oil, small sauces) are costed when bought.

Recipes

Components are cooked in batches and shared (rice, sauces, the fried chicken, rendang). Dishes are one serve of a Square item, built from components and ingredients, plus packaging. Tap Made a batch each time a component is cooked: it teaches the tool how many serves a batch really gives.

Menu costs

Square sales in the range times each dish's recipe cost: the expected food cost. Prices are what customers actually paid, ex GST, after discounts (staff meals included). Real food cost from stocktakes comes in the next part.

Suppliers
